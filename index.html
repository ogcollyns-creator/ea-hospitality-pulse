About

Intelligence, not a news feed

Anyone can forward a headline. EA Hospitality Pulse exists to answer the only question that matters to an owner: so what should I do about it?

Every trading day we scan official tourism boards and statistics bureaus, the region's reputable press, and global market-intelligence sources — then filter for what actually moves demand, supply, cost, risk or reputation for East African properties. Each item is tagged by segment (city, bush, beach), country, and how confident we are, and closes with a concrete move: hold rate, lock the contract, repackage, redirect the marketing spend.

We publish three times a day, plus a Sunday Foresight edition that turns the week's signals into a 30/90-day plan. When our foresight is later confirmed by the data, we say so — because credibility is the product.

How we work. Every claim is attributed to a named source and graded by confidence. Every forecast is logged in our public track record with a falsifiable test — including the calls we get wrong. Corrections are published, not quietly edited.
