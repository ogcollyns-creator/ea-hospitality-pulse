# Contributing to EA Hospitality Pulse

Two different kinds of contribution are welcome, and they work differently — pick the one that matches what you want to submit.

## 1. A guest guide or op-ed

For a standalone, bylined piece — a market view, an operator's perspective, a "here's what I'm seeing" — that runs in the [Guides](https://eahospitalitypulse.com/#guides) section under your own name.

1. Fork this repository.
2. Copy [`templates/guide-template.md`](templates/guide-template.md) into `guides-src/` as a new file (e.g. `guides-src/your-slug-here.md`) and fill it in. The template explains the frontmatter fields and the house rules (sourced claims, category, your byline).
3. Open a pull request. Fill in the PR checklist — it's short.
4. An editor reviews it. Nothing publishes until it's approved and merged; you'll get review comments in the PR if something needs a source or a rewrite.
5. Once merged, the site rebuilds itself automatically — your piece is live within a couple of minutes, with your name on it.

You don't need to be added as a collaborator for this — anyone with a GitHub account can fork and open a PR.

## 2. A tip for the daily Pulse

For something you think should be *in* an upcoming Morning, Midday, Evening or Sunday Foresight edition — a rule change, a fee update, a route shift, anything that moves demand, cost, risk or reputation.

The daily edition has its own strict internal format (it posts automatically to Telegram, WhatsApp and LinkedIn the moment it's published), so tips don't go in as a file — they go in as a [GitHub Issue using the Pulse story tip template](../../issues/new?template=pulse-tip.yml). Give a source and a date; unsourced tips won't run. An editor decides whether and how it's folded into the next edition.

## House rules, either way

- **Every factual claim needs a named, checkable source.** A government notice, an official report, a dated article — not "I heard."
- **Corrections get published, not quietly edited.** If something you contributed turns out to be wrong, say so and we'll fix it visibly.
- **Editorial independence isn't for sale.** No advertorial, no pay-for-placement coverage dressed up as editorial.
