**Tanzania raised its October fuel price caps on 7 October: Dar es Salaam petrol is now TSh 4,057 a litre, up 6.9%**
_🌆 Evening Wrap · Thu 8 Oct 2026_

━━━━━━━━━
1️⃣ Tanzania's fuel caps rose 5–10% for October, with Kyerwa petrol at TSh 4,370

EWURA's October maximum retail prices took effect 7 Oct. Dar es Salaam: petrol TSh 4,057 (+261 from 3,796), diesel 4,086 (+209 from 3,877), kerosene 4,067 (+354 from 3,713). Tanga petrol is 4,123, Mtwara 4,170, Kyerwa (Kagera) 4,370. EWURA cites renewed Middle East tensions *(Daily News, 8 Oct, reporting EWURA)*. Our arithmetic: petrol +6.9%, diesel +5.4%, kerosene +9.5%.
Our inference: the lodge cost is distance from port, not Dar. Fuel to Serengeti, Kagera and Ruaha camps carries a premium on top, and game-drive diesel is the line that moves.
🎯 So what: re-cost October-December vehicle and generator runs at the October caps before you issue 2027 contract rates.
🏷 City, Bush, Beach | Tanzania | Confirmed | impact:margin

2️⃣ Uganda's Parliament pressed the government on 7 October to explain rising pump prices

Minister Musenero attributed the rise to the shilling moving from about Shs3,600 to Shs4,000 per dollar, a Shs200 excise increase in the 2026/27 budget and global supply pressure. MPs asked for UNOC's selling-price breakdown, suspension of the excise rise and tax relief. She had not received the breakdown and the debate was adjourned *(Parliament of Uganda, 8 Oct)*. One MP asked for supply-disruption planning tied to instability in Kenya.
🎯 So what: a weaker shilling hits imported food and beverage costs as well as fuel. Do not lock Uganda dollar-priced rates for 2027 until the statement on the economy lands.
🏷 City, Bush | Uganda | Reported | impact:margin

3️⃣ WHO's Tedros advised against closing Kenya's borders on 7 October; EAC health ministers record no border measures

Tedros told CS Duale that screening and contact tracing work better than blanket restrictions *(Kenyans.co.ke, 8 Oct)*. Kenya counts 10 people in quarantine and 57 contacts being traced, including passengers and crew. A suspected case in Wajir, reported by The Standard, has no published lab result. The EAC health ministers' communiqué of 8 Oct records no border measures and no case counts *(eac.int)*.
🎯 So what: stay with the line "imported case, no local transmission". Do not repeat the Wajir report to agents until a result is published.
🏷 City, Bush, Beach | Kenya | Reported | impact:risk

## LINKEDIN

Fuel is the cost line that moved this week, in two countries at once.

On 7 October Tanzania's EWURA set October maximum retail prices: Dar es Salaam petrol at TSh 4,057 a litre, up 261 on September's 3,796 (6.9%), diesel at 4,086, up 209 (5.4%). Prices rise with distance from port: Mtwara petrol is 4,170, and Kyerwa in Kagera 4,370. EWURA cites renewed Middle East tensions.

The same day in Uganda, the Minister told Parliament that the pump price reflects a shilling that has moved from about Shs3,600 to Shs4,000 per dollar, a Shs200 excise increase and global supply pressure. MPs asked for UNOC's selling-price breakdown and tax relief. The debate was adjourned without one.

The inference is ours. Bush operators carry the premium twice: fuel is dearest where the roads are longest, and game-drive diesel is the line guests never see on a rate card. A weaker shilling then lifts imported food and beverage costs too.

Re-cost the October to December vehicle and generator runs at the new caps before 2027 contract rates go out. In Kenya, the 8% fuel VAT ends on 14 October, the same day EPRA reviews prices.
