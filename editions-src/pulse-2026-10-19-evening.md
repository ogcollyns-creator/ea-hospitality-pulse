1️⃣ Kenya's Ebola count reached eight on 18 October after a Nairobi Hospital patient attendant tested positive

The Ministry of Health tested 38 contacts on Sunday and one was positive: a 49-year-old man who attended the first patient and was already in quarantine. Director General Patrick Amoth says he is not a direct health worker, is isolated, and that the other confirmed patients have received monoclonal antibody treatment. This follows seven cases on 17 Oct, three of them health workers. The Ministry still reports no community transmission and no restriction on travel, trade or gatherings *(Ministry of Health via Kenyans.co.ke, 19 Oct; Kenyans.co.ke, 17 Oct)*.
Our inference: the count rose four times between 14 and 18 Oct, always inside quarantine, so the 21-day clock can keep resetting into November.
🎯 So what: keep cancellation terms steady and brief front desk staff on the Ministry's wording.

2️⃣ DRC's Ebola outbreak reached an eighth province, Nord-Ubangi, on 19 October, with at least 9,400 cases and 4,500 deaths

Nord-Ubangi borders the Central African Republic and reported two cases, one fatal. The count is DW with AP and Reuters on 19 Oct. Our 12 Oct edition carried 8,978 cases and 4,336 deaths to 10 Oct from the WHO count; the two series differ in method, so treat the gap as direction, not rate *(DW via allAfrica, 19 Oct)*.
Our inference: a northern province points away from the Uganda and Rwanda corridors, but source-market caution tracks the total, not the geography.
🎯 So what: expect rule changes to keep coming from third countries; log each one before you quote guests.
🏷 City, Bush, Beach | Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, Tanzania | Reported | impact:watch

3️⃣ Kenya's matatu owners ruled out a fuel strike on 18 October, but a nationwide cost-of-living protest is planned for 20 October

Matatu Owners Association president Albert Karakacha said operators will absorb diesel at KSh227.76 a litre, up KSh9 from 15 Oct. The Counties Commuters Welfare Association gave EPRA 14 days to reverse the rises or face court, and activists planned a protest on Tuesday *(Kenyans.co.ke, 19 Oct)*.
Our inference: protest days can disrupt Nairobi roads at short notice.
🎯 So what: add buffer to Tuesday airport transfers and confirm staff commutes.

## LINKEDIN

Kenya's Ebola count is now eight, and the pattern matters more than the number.

The latest case, confirmed on 18 October, is a 49-year-old patient attendant at Nairobi Hospital who was already in quarantine. The Ministry tested 38 contacts that day and one was positive. Director General Patrick Amoth says the other confirmed patients have received monoclonal antibody treatment. On 17 October three health workers were among four new cases. The Ministry still reports no community transmission and no restriction on travel, trade or gatherings.

Every case so far surfaced inside quarantine, which is monitoring doing its job. But each contact is watched for 21 days from their own last exposure, so a late case can move the end date into November.
