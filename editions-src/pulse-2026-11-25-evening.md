## LINKEDIN

Africa's health agencies are planning for Ebola to last through the next high season.

Africa CDC and WHO posted their joint Bundibugyo virus plan today. It runs from November 2026 to May 2027 and is costed at US$1.576bn. As of 17 November it counted 11,613 confirmed cases and 5,678 deaths in Congo, in eight provinces. WHO rates the risk high in Kenya and Nigeria.

Our read: the people paying for the response expect it to cover January to March and Easter. Uganda declared its own outbreak over in July. Kenya's countdown ends on 23 December if no new case appears. Neither date stops a nervous agent in Chicago or Frankfurt from asking what happens if a case turns up in February.

That question is cheaper to answer in a contract than on the phone. Lodges in Uganda and Kenya signing January–May 2027 business should name the trigger now: a new confirmed case in your country, and what you will refund or move if it happens. Agents sell what they can explain to a client in one sentence.
