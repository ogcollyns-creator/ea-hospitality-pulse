## LINKEDIN

Europe has now logged four imported Ebola cases, and not one traveller among them. That distinction decides what you tell guests.

ECDC reported on 1 October that a healthcare worker based in the DRC tested positive on 29 September and was evacuated to the Netherlands. The three earlier imports were also medical or humanitarian workers, and ECDC rates the risk to Europe's general population as very low. DRC's Health Ministry puts the outbreak at 8,300 cases and 4,018 deaths, while weekly cases averaged about 490 in September against about 850 in mid-July. Uganda's outbreak was declared over on 27 August.

My reading, and it is inference: the epidemiology is easing, but headlines reach your source markets faster than case data does. Guests booking Uganda or Rwanda will ask, and a vague answer costs more than a precise one.

What an owner can do now. Add two lines to pre-arrival emails: no case in East Africa, and Kenya screens arrivals at Nairobi and Mombasa airports. Keep flexible terms for guests who also touch the US, because that entry order runs to 11 October. Log every Ebola question your reservations team gets this month; it is free demand data.

Two other risks sit alongside: a flydubai cockpit attack on 30 September with a US military build-up behind it, and Ethiopian's first international flights into Lamu's Manda airport.
