## LINKEDIN

A country's all-clear does not end another country's border measure.

On 28 September Canada extended its Ebola-related suspension of immigration documents by 60 days, to 27 November. It still covers people whose last country of residence is Uganda, alongside the DRC and South Sudan. CP24 reports Uganda's own outbreak was declared over in late August. Two days earlier, on 26 September, the UAE eased its curbs on Ugandans.

So Uganda now sits on different lists in different source markets. That is a distinction owners can act on.

My reading, and it is inference: each government is keyed to the DRC outbreak and its own politics, not to Uganda's case count. The WHO's 30 September update describes a response still scaling up in Ituri, with a US$1.3bn, 180-day plan.

What to do this week: list your top five source markets and write down, per market, which measure applies and when it is next reviewed. Brief reservations staff on the difference between a rule on travellers to a country and a rule on residents of one. Keep Uganda-resident staff and partners out of any Canada-bound itinerary until 27 November.

Two dated items sit alongside it. Tanzania's US$44 inbound insurance starts tomorrow, and the NIC FAQ ties the exemption to residence, not nationality. Kenya's September inflation rose to 6.8%, with transport at 15.6%.
