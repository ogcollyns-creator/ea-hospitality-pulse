## LINKEDIN BIG READ

**Nobody will cancel a booking because of AI search. They will simply never hear of you.**

Your pick-up report will not show this. That is precisely what makes it dangerous.

**THE SITUATION**

Between January and April 2026, 68.01% of Google searches in the United States ended without a single click to anywhere — up from 60.45% in 2024, per SparkToro's analysis of Similarweb clickstream data, published 9 June 2026. AI Overviews now appear on more than 20% of searches, and where one appears, click-through to the results below falls by close to 60%.

The largest travel company in the world confirmed the mechanism three weeks ago. On Booking Holdings' second-quarter call, chief executive Glenn Fogel told analysts that Google's display changes "definitely put some pressure on SEO", while noting that direct traffic — in the mid-60% range — "hasn't gone down at all" (Skift, 4 August 2026). Room nights grew 5%. Net income rose 118% to US$2bn. The business is fine.

Nor is traveller appetite theoretical. Skift Research's State of Travel 2026 puts familiarity with AI trip-planning tools at 62% of global travellers, with usage up 124% year on year (reported by Open Jaw, 13 August 2026).

**WHAT EVERYONE IS MISSING**

Read the Booking split again. It contains the whole argument. Direct traffic held. Search-led discovery did not.

Direct traffic is people who already know your name. Discovery is people who do not. When the search box stops sending clicks, a global brand loses one marketing channel among many. A fourteen-tent camp in the Mara loses the only one it ever had.

East Africa is unusually exposed, and not for the reason most people assume. A safari is the most heavily researched purchase in leisure travel. Nobody books a bush camp on impulse. They spend weeks on informational queries: when the crossing happens, what a gorilla permit costs, whether Zanzibar is safe, Kenya or Tanzania. Those are exactly the questions a generated answer settles on the page, without a click.

The damage also does not land where you are watching for it. Most East African bed-nights arrive through a tour operator or an agent. When the research layer collapses, the enquiry that never happened is absorbed by your agent, not by you. You will see a normal quarter. Your agent will see a thinner funnel. The gap between those two facts is three to six months of lead time nobody here is using.

**WHAT TO DO ABOUT IT**

Publish facts, not atmosphere. Generated answers quote specific, dated, checkable statements. A page promising "unforgettable moments under an endless sky" cannot be quoted by anything. A page that states the KWS conservation fee schedule effective 1 October 2025, with the figure and the date, can be — and will be. Move your fee tables, exact seasonality windows, transfer times in minutes, permit prices and the notices they came from into plain, dated, structured pages. It is the cheapest item on this list by a wide margin.

City hotels: your queries are transactional and branded — "hotels near KICC", or your own name. That is the least damaged segment, and Fogel's own evidence supports it. Keep your Google Business Profile, rate parity and review corpus clean, because those are what a model reads when someone asks it where to stay in Westlands.

Bush and beach: you carry the exposure. Your traffic was informational and your name is not yet known. Own the factual questions in your area — park rules, fee changes, crossing dates, permit logistics — with more current and better-dated answers than any aggregator publishes.

Then change the number you watch. Ask your three largest agents, in writing, for monthly enquiry counts by source market, not bookings. Enquiries move first. If that line falls while your pick-up holds, you have a discovery problem, not a rate problem — and cutting rate will not fix it.

**THE WATCH**

Booking Holdings' Q3 results, due late October, and whether Fogel repeats, escalates or withdraws the SEO comment. SparkToro's next zero-click read. And Google's own disclosure on AI Mode, which the company said at I/O 2026 had passed one billion monthly users with query volume more than doubling each quarter — it accounted for just 0.34% of searches in the January-to-April window, so its effect sits ahead of us, not behind.

The properties that come through this will not be the ones with the best photography. They will be the ones a machine can quote.
