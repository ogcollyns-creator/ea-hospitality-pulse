## LINKEDIN

Uganda's gorilla lodges have a date to watch that is not on most calendars: 27 November.

That is when Canada's Ebola border rules expire, unless Ottawa renews them. Foreign nationals who were in Uganda or South Sudan in the previous 21 days can enter Canada but must quarantine for 21 days. Canadian citizens returning from Uganda quarantine too, and transit counts. Separately, the immigration department has suspended visas and eTAs for residents of Uganda to the same date (Public Health Agency of Canada page, modified 4 November; IRCC, 28 September). Ottawa also still advises against non-essential travel to all of Uganda.

Uganda declared its own outbreak over in the summer. The rules exist because of the DRC, where Africa CDC said on 15 November the outbreak is still not under control.

Our read: renewal is more likely than lapse. Canada extended for 60 days in September, and the US renewed its own order to 10 December.

For operators, the practical step is simple. Tell Canadian agents the rules stand until at least 27 November, quote no lifting date, and build itineraries that respect the 21-day window.
