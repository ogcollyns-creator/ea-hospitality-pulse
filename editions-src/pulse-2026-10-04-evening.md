1️⃣ TANZANIA OPENS A CUSTOMS AMNESTY FOR MOTOR VEHICLES FROM 2 OCTOBER TO 31 DECEMBER

The Tanzania Revenue Authority's public notice, issued in Dar es Salaam on 2 October, lets owners of vehicles that breached customs rules disclose and regularise them at any TRA office, paying all applicable duties and taxes *(TRA public notice, 2 Oct)*. Covered breaches include misuse of temporary importation, a misstated year or model, altered VIN or chassis numbers, misapplied SADC origin rules, tax-exemption abuse and unauthorised structural alteration. After 31 December, TRA says it will pursue enforcement against non-compliant vehicles *(TRA, 2 Oct)*. The notice does not mention tourism. *Our inference:* lodges, DMCs and transfer operators running imported or modified vehicles in Tanzania should audit their own paperwork before year-end, because the deadline falls in peak season.

The listed breaches include misuse of temporary importation, a misstated manufacturing year or model, altered VIN or chassis numbers and unauthorised structural alteration.
