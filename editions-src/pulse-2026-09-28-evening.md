## LINKEDIN

Uganda's Ebola curbs are easing. The outbreak that caused all of them just became the worst the Democratic Republic of Congo has ever recorded.

The UAE lifted its entry restrictions on Ugandan nationals on Saturday, citing Uganda's completed 21-day monitoring period. The DRC and South Sudan remain restricted. That distinction matters more than the relief headlines suggest: the World Health Organization's latest Disease Outbreak News, published 25 September, puts DRC's confirmed case count at 7,890, with 3,799 deaths — the largest Ebola outbreak the country has ever recorded, still spreading into new health zones.

Canada's own temporary border measures, extended once already, are due to expire tonight, 28 September, at 23:59 EDT. As of this afternoon, no renewal had been posted. WHO's official advice is against any travel or trade restriction at all. Nobody enforcing one has followed it.

For operators, the read is simple: don't treat Uganda-specific easing as the start of a broader wind-down. Every restriction reaching your guests is tied to DRC's numbers, not Uganda's — and DRC's numbers just got worse.
