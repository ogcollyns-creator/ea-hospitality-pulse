1️⃣ The UK added Moyale to its Kenya "all but essential" list on 6 November

**What happened.** The FCDO updated its Kenya advice on 6 Nov (17:00 UTC) to cover Moyale Town and up to 5km around it, including the Ethiopia border. Its regional-risks page cites violence in the Ethiopian town of Moyale since October, with stray gunfire and projectiles landing within 3km *(gov.uk, 6 Nov)*. About 50,000 people have crossed into Kenya by local authorities' count; UNHCR counts at least 11,000 in temporary sites *(Africanews/AFP, 5 Nov)*.

**Read.** Inference: no safari or coast area is touched, and the level elsewhere is unchanged. The risk is overland Ethiopia–Kenya itineraries via Marsabit, which the FCDO tells vehicles to run straight down the A2.

🎯 So what: if you sell Ethiopia–Kenya overland add-ons, pull them. Brief reservations that this is local to the border.

3️⃣ Kenya's Ebola 42-day clock has not started: one patient is still in treatment (Health Ministry, 6 Nov)

Nine cases, two deaths, six recoveries; last infection 19 Oct. The 42 days start only when the last patient leaves treatment clear of the virus. 52 contacts are due out by 11 Nov *(Standard, Kenyans.co.ke, 6 Nov)*. We found no discharge since. By our arithmetic, a discharge today would end the countdown on 20 Dec.
🎯 So what: tie December cancellation terms to "42 days after the last patient", not the 11 Nov contact release.

## LINKEDIN

The UK has drawn a new line inside Kenya, and it is nowhere near a safari circuit.

On 6 November the FCDO advised against all but essential travel to Moyale Town and the area up to 5km around it, including the Ethiopia border. Its advice cites violence in the Ethiopian town of Moyale since October, with stray gunfire and projectiles landing within 3km. Local authorities say about 50,000 people have crossed into Kenya.

The level elsewhere in Kenya is unchanged. For a lodge or city hotel the exposure is narrow: overland Ethiopia–Kenya itineraries through Marsabit. The FCDO tells vehicles to stay on the A2 and go straight through.

The bigger decision this week is in Washington. The US entry order on travellers recently in the DRC, Uganda and South Sudan lapses on 10 November unless renewed, and we found no renewal notice. Earlier orders ran in 30-day steps. If Uganda stays listed after 27 weeks Ebola-free, gorilla-season bookings from US agents stay exposed.
