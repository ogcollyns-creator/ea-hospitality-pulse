## LINKEDIN

A travel advisory that several outlets are reporting as "renewed 28 July 2026" is, when you open the actual page, dated 17 March 2025.

We fetched the US State Department's Kenya advisory directly at midday today. Kenya remains Level 2. The Nairobi neighbourhoods of Eastleigh and Kibera sit under "Reconsider Travel" — not the "Do Not Travel" designation now circulating on several secondary sites.

The gap matters commercially. Corporate risk teams increasingly assemble country guidance from search results, and search ranks by relevance, not recency. The Citizen Digital article currently surfacing for this story was published on 18 March 2025; its page was re-touched today, which is enough to make it read as breaking news.

For an operator in Nairobi, the practical exposure is a client's travel-risk desk downgrading the city on the strength of a headline describing a document nobody has revised in 503 days.

The discipline is unglamorous: open the primary source and read the date on it. A claimed date is a claim. An observed one is evidence.
