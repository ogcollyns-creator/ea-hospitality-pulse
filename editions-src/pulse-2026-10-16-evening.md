1️⃣ Kenya confirmed a second Ebola case on 14 October, a 39-year-old relative already in quarantine

Symptoms began 13 Oct; two labs confirmed it. Kenya now has two cases and one death. The first patient had 128 listed contacts: 113 traced, 108 in quarantine, 5 outside Kenya, 15 still being traced. The Ministry sees no wider community spread and no restriction on travel, trade or gatherings *(Ministry of Health via Pulse Kenya and Medical Daily, 14–15 Oct)*.
Our inference: a case inside quarantine is the system working, but contacts are watched for 21 days, so the count can move into November.
🎯 So what: keep staff briefings factual and watch the Public Health PS's suspension of government meetings in hotels.
🏷 City, Bush, Beach | Kenya | Confirmed | impact:risk

2️⃣ The US lowered Tanzania from Level 3 to Level 2 on 16 October

It had been Level 3, "reconsider travel", since 31 Oct 2025. Level 2 cites crime, terrorism, health (a new indicator), unrest and arbitrary law enforcement. Mtwara, on the Mozambique border, stays at "reconsider travel" *(travel.state.gov, 16 Oct)*.
Our inference: the page is country-wide, so Zanzibar should inherit it; we saw no separate line.
🎯 So what: US-market sellers can drop the Level 3 caveat from Tanzania quotes now.
🏷 Bush, Beach | Tanzania, Zanzibar | Confirmed | impact:demand

3️⃣ Kenya's EPRA lifted pump prices from 15 October: petrol KSh226.96, diesel KSh227.76, kerosene KSh219.85

Rises of KSh12.93, KSh9.90 and KSh28.49 respectively, valid to 14 Nov. EPRA's director-general Edward Kinyua blamed the Middle East conflict for higher import costs *(The Star, 14 Oct)*. Diesel now sits above petrol. The VAT extension bill's outcome is not confirmed.
🎯 So what: re-cost transfer and generator budgets at about KSh228 a litre of diesel until 14 Nov.
🏷 City, Bush, Beach | Kenya | Confirmed | impact:margin

## LINKEDIN

On Friday the US State Department lowered Tanzania from Level 3 to Level 2, ending a year at "reconsider travel" that began on 31 October 2025.

The new advisory still lists crime, terrorism, unrest and arbitrary enforcement of laws, and it adds a health indicator. The Mtwara border area with Mozambique stays at "reconsider travel". The page is country-wide, so Zanzibar should inherit the change, although we have not seen a separate Zanzibar line.

The commercial effect is slower than the headline. A level change does not rebook a safari. What it does is remove the first objection a US agent or corporate travel manager has to raise, and it lands as the high season approaches.

Set it against the other risk on the board. Kenya confirmed its second Ebola case on 14 October, a quarantined relative of the first patient. The Ministry reports 108 contacts in quarantine, no community spread and no restrictions. Two markets, two directions on risk, in the same week.

For operators: update Tanzania quotes and sales scripts, brief front-of-house on Kenya's facts, and re-cost diesel at about KSh228 a litre in Kenya until 14 November.
