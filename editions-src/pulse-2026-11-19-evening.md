1️⃣ JKIA tour-van and shuttle permits could rise by up to 66% under KAA's draft charges

**What happened.** KAA's draft Commercial Charges Order, 2026 would lift six-month JKIA stickers for airport-based tour operators and shuttles: saloons and SUVs KSh12,060 to KSh15,000, vans KSh12,060 to KSh20,000, shuttles up to 25 seats KSh25,056 to KSh40,000. Ride-hailing cabs would pay KSh150–250 per JKIA trip; 12–24 hours in the silo goes from KSh1,800 to KSh2,250 *(The Star; Kenyans.co.ke, 19 Nov)*. Nairobi forum Tue 24 Nov, Panari Hotel; written comments by 1 Dec *(KAA notice via Kenyans.co.ke, 23 Oct)*.

**Read.** It is a draft with no effective date. Our arithmetic: the tour-operator and shuttle stickers rise 24% to 66%. Inference: 2027 transfer rates set before the draft do not include it.

🎯 So what: re-cost 2027 transfer rates now, and send KAA your numbers before 1 Dec.

## LINKEDIN

Kenya's airport transfer costs are about to be repriced, and owners have until 1 December to say something.

Kenya Airports Authority's draft Commercial Charges Order, 2026, reported by The Star and Kenyans.co.ke on 19 November, would raise the six-month JKIA sticker for an airport-based tour van from KSh12,060 to KSh20,000. Shuttles of up to 25 seats go from KSh25,056 to KSh40,000. Ride-hailing cabs would pay KSh150 to 250 for every completed JKIA trip.

None of this is in force yet. There is no effective date, and KAA is holding a public forum in Nairobi on 24 November, with written comments due on 1 December.

Our read: an airport sticker is a small line on its own, but it lands on top of fuel caps that have been held up by subsidy and a Treasury plan to toll Thika Road and the Southern Bypass. If your 2027 transfer rates were set before these drafts appeared, they do not include them.

The practical step is simple. Re-cost every transfer you sell or subcontract, then send KAA the numbers. Regulators move on submissions with figures in them, not on complaints after the order is gazetted.
