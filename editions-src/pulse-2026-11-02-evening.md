1️⃣ Uganda bought itself a seat at the 2027 AFCON, and the hotel work starts with packages

**What happened.** The Uganda Tourism Board signed an official destination sponsorship agreement with the Confederation of African Football at the Kampala Marriott on Friday 30 October. "Explore Uganda – The Pearl of Africa" will run on AFCON broadcasts, digital content and in-stadium advertising. UTB is the first regional sponsor named for the tournament, which Uganda co-hosts with Kenya and Tanzania in June 2027, the first three-nation AFCON *(New Vision, 31 Oct)*.

**What was not said.** The article gives no hotel figures, occupancy targets or booking projections. UTB plans match-plus-wildlife, culture and food packages and a "HERE FOR IT" campaign "in the coming weeks"; the State Minister for Tourism told operators to build competitive packages; the Deputy Speaker called for investment in accommodation, transport and security *(New Vision, 31 Oct)*.
Our inference: the sponsorship buys visibility, not rooms. Only operators with a priced package can convert it.

🎯 So what: if you are in Kampala, Entebbe or on the gorilla and Murchison circuits, price a match-plus-safari package now and hold June 2027 inventory back from net-rate contracts until UTB publishes its campaign details.
