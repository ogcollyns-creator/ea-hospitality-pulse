1️⃣ Kenya has 108 of 122 Ebola contacts in quarantine, none symptomatic

Of 293 samples from 289 alerts, only the confirmed case is positive. 667,675 travellers have been screened at 26 entry points. 241 isolation beds sit across five hospitals in Nairobi, Eldoret and Mombasa *(Kenyans.co.ke, 11 Oct)*. Our inference: the suspension is unlikely to lift before the contact quarantine ends; no end date is published.

Our inference: capacity recovery is possible but not yet funded in cash.

## LINKEDIN

Kenya's Ebola response now has numbers, and they point to a long tail for hotels rather than a spike.

On Sunday 11 October Health CS Aden Duale said all 122 contacts of Kenya's single case are listed and verified. 108 of them, 88.5%, are in quarantine and monitored daily, and none has shown symptoms. Of 293 samples tested, only the confirmed case is positive. 667,675 travellers have been screened at 26 entry points.

The commercial effect so far is administrative. The Public Health PS suspended government meetings and seminars in hotels on 7 October, "until further notice", and nothing published since lifts it.

The inference is ours: that suspension probably outlasts the contact quarantine, because no end date has been set.

Two practical steps. Get hold, move or cancel terms in writing from any ministry or agency with a block on your books, and check your force-majeure clause. And if you sell Uganda and Kenya together to US guests, keep flexible terms this week: the CDC entry order on Uganda expires tonight, and its last two renewals were published five days after the expiry date.
