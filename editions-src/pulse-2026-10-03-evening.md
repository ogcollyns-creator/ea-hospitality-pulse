## LINKEDIN

Tanzania's US$44 insurance rule is now two days old, and the likeliest failure is the wrong policy rather than none.

The rule comes from Government Notice No. 256 of 4 September 2026. It charges US$44 per visitor, valid up to 92 days with multiple entries. For a trip that combines the Mainland and Zanzibar, ATTA reports that one policy is required, based on the first point of entry, and that Zanzibar entry uses a separate portal. The UK FCDO added the rule to its Tanzania advice on 1 October.

We looked for reports of airport queues, portal failures or airline check-in refusals since 1 October and found none. That is absence of evidence only, and the first peak-season weekends are the real test.

My reading, and it is inference: the exposure sits with the lodge or DMC that sold the safari, because the guest will blame whoever briefed them last.

Three things an operator can do now. Tell guests in writing to buy for their first point of entry. Ask them to keep the certificate or QR code on their phone. State whether your package price includes the US$44, so it does not surface as an argument at the front desk.
