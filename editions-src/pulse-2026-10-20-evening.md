2️⃣ Kenya's public health workers' strike reached 90 days on 19 October, with nationwide demonstrations threatened from Wednesday 21 October

The Kenya Environmental Health and Public Health Practitioners Union gave government 48 hours to end the strike. It wants permanent jobs for contract staff, higher risk allowances and signed pay deals honoured. Leaders warn the strike undermines Ebola containment, with about 4,200 public health officers serving nearly 50 million people *(Kenyans.co.ke, 19 Oct, 19:50 EAT)*. A separate fuel-price protest call tied to Mashujaa Day, 20 Oct, names no date or route and no police statement; matatu owners say they will not strike *(The Star, 20 Oct)*.
Our inference: two protest calls in 48 hours raise the odds of Nairobi road delays.
🎯 So what: add buffer to Wednesday airport transfers and confirm staff commutes tonight.
🏷 City | Kenya | Reported | impact:risk

3️⃣ Zanzibar's airline seats are forecast to rise 33% over six months while online-agency room rates fell about 18% in the year to October

ATTA released Data Appeal Mabrian figures at its Essence of Africa buyer forum in Zanzibar, 20 to 22 Oct. Domestic seats are up 36% and international 31% year on year; forward GDS bookings to March 2027 are up 10%; UK bookings are up 51%. The release gives no arrivals figures and does not explain how the 33% was built *(VoyagesAfriq, 20 Oct; ATTA)*.
Our inference: seats are growing faster than forward bookings, so the 18% rate drop may continue. The bases differ, so this is direction only.
🎯 So what: hold December to March rates, and check parity before releasing allotments.

## LINKEDIN

The US has moved Kenya from Level 2 to Level 3, and the grounds matter as much as the number.

The State Department made the change on 19 October. Its summary leads with health, after eight confirmed Ebola cases, and keeps crime, kidnapping, terrorism, unrest and road safety. The Level 4 areas did not change: Eastleigh, Kibera, the north-east and north coast, West Pokot and Turkana. Nairobi's hotel districts, Diani and Watamu are not in them.

The UK Foreign Office re-dated its Kenya page on 16 October for Ebola screening but did not change its level, and Canada's 15 October update kept "high degree of caution". Different source markets now see different Kenyas.

Our inference, not a documented rule: Level 3 may trigger review clauses at some insurers and corporate travel desks. Kenya now sits above Tanzania, which the US lowered to Level 2 on 16 October.

For owners: ask your US agents which policies they apply before they ask you, offer date changes instead of rate cuts, and send US guests the Level 4 map. Discounting into a risk perception rarely restores the booking.
