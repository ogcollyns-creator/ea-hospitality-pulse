1️⃣ Tanzania out-earns Kenya on visitors by US$585m a year, with fewer of them

**What happened.** An EAC Secretariat report puts 2025 regional travel earnings at US$11.99bn. Tanzania took US$4.41bn from about 2.3m foreign visitors; Kenya US$3.83bn from about 2.6m. Uganda was third (US$1.78bn), Rwanda fifth (US$604m). Travel is 55% of EAC service exports, from 31% in 2020 *(EAC Secretariat via KT Press, 17 Nov)*.

**Read.** Our arithmetic: about US$1,920 per visitor in Tanzania against about US$1,470 in Kenya. Inference: park fees, longer stays and Zanzibar beach extensions widen the gap, not room rates alone.

🎯 So what: Kenya owners, sell the add-on night, not a cheaper rate. Tanzania owners, show lenders this figure.

3️⃣ Dubai airport now has 57 airlines, half last year's count

DXB handled 50.4m passengers in January–September, down 28.1%, and expects "more than 70 million" for 2026 *(Dubai Airports, 17 Nov)*. That compares with 95.2m in 2025 and 108 airlines a year ago *(Skift, 17 Nov)*. Many carriers left after the US-Iran war, and Europe's conflict-zone bulletin runs to 31 Jan 2027.

**Read.** Inference: Emirates and flydubai still fly, but thinner interline choice pushes Europe and Asia traffic to Addis, Istanbul and nonstops.
🎯 So what: find agent itineraries still routed via Dubai and quote nonstop or Addis options.

## LINKEDIN

Kenya draws more visitors than Tanzania. Tanzania earns more from them.

An East African Community Secretariat report, reported by KT Press on 17 November, puts the region's 2025 travel earnings at US$11.99 billion. Tanzania took US$4.41 billion from about 2.3 million foreign visitors. Kenya took US$3.83 billion from about 2.6 million.

On our arithmetic, that is roughly US$1,920 per visitor in Tanzania against US$1,470 in Kenya. The gap is about 30%.

Our read: it is not mainly a room-rate story. Tanzania charges more at the park gate, its safari itineraries tend to run longer, and Zanzibar adds beach nights to many trips. Kenya's strengths, short-haul access, Nairobi business travel and the shorter Mara break, bring more people for less per head.

For owners the useful question is what you can add, not what you can cut. A third night in the Mara, a coast extension sold at the same time as the safari, a conference-plus-safari package for Nairobi delegates. Each moves a guest from the Kenya average towards the Tanzania one.

Travel is now 55% of the EAC's service export earnings, up from 31% in 2020. Lenders and ministries are reading these numbers. Owners should too.
