## LINKEDIN

Lamu is about to get two economies at once, and few owners there have chosen between them.

On 30 September, President Ruto and Aliko Dangote broke ground on a refinery beside Lamu Port: KSh2.2tn, about US$16bn, 700,000 barrels a day, with operation targeted for 2030. The Star reports backers claiming 60,000 direct jobs. It also reports 133 Chandavai residents suing over land, with the High Court declining to halt construction.

My reading, and it is inference: a decade of construction brings a long-stay contractor market for Mokowe, Lamu town and Manda. It also puts heavy industry within reach of a beach and heritage product that sells on quiet. I have not read an environmental licence, so I cannot say how the two will coexist.

What an owner can do now. Decide which segment the property serves, because the same rooms rarely sell to both. Ask NEMA for the licence status and any conditions. Price 2028 stock with a clause that lets you move rates if the roads, ferry or power change.

Two dated items sit alongside. Tanzania's US$44 inbound insurance is in force from today. The US entry ban on non-citizens recently in Uganda expires on 11 October unless CDC renews it.
