_🌆 Evening Wrap · Sun 25 Oct 2026_

1️⃣ Kenya's first Ebola contacts were due out of quarantine on 25 October, but the 42-day clock to an all-clear has not started

Health Director General Dr Patrick Amoth said on Thursday 22 Oct that "our first contacts will begin leaving the quarantine facilities on Sunday", after the full 21-day monitoring period without symptoms. The Ministry puts the count at nine cases, with 134 of 135 contacts traced as of 20 Oct. It also said the 42-day countdown to being declared Ebola-free has not formally begun *(Kenyans.co.ke, 22 Oct 14:00)*. We have not yet seen a Ministry statement confirming that releases happened today.
Our inference: counting 42 days from the ninth case (confirmed 20 Oct) gives about 1 December at the very earliest, and the 23 Oct death pushes that later. "Cases are contained" and "Kenya is declared clear" are different dates.
🎯 So what: brief reception on the first, not the second. Do not promise guests an all-clear before December.

2️⃣ Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania have still not agreed a joint AFCON 2027 visa; the proposal is a four-month visa with fee and dates unconfirmed
KATA's 25 Oct note says the plan is "not a final tripartite agreement", with fee, eligibility and operating dates unset, and the tournament running 19 June to 17 July 2027. Kenya's Sports CS Salim Mvurya announced the "PAMOJA Visa Framework" on 25 Aug, with no dates or fees *(Kenyans.co.ke, 25 Aug)*. Uganda's Cabinet separately backed a three-month visa-fee waiver around the tournament, announced 7 Apr *(VisasNews, 7 Apr; secondary)*.
Our inference: a June-July 2027 rate card cannot assume a single-visa gateway yet. Only Uganda has a stated fee relief, and that is reported second-hand.
🎯 So what: price AFCON-window rooms on the current visa rules. Add a visa-change clause rather than discounting for an unconfirmed benefit.

## LINKEDIN

Kenya's Ebola contacts start going home today. The date everyone is waiting for is still weeks away.

On Thursday the Health Director General said the first contacts would leave quarantine on Sunday 25 October, after 21 days without symptoms. The Ministry counts nine cases and had traced 134 of 135 contacts by 20 October. It also said the 42-day countdown to being declared Ebola-free has not formally begun (Kenyans.co.ke, 22 October).

Our inference: if the countdown ran from the ninth case, confirmed on 20 October, the earliest declaration would be about 1 December. A death on 23 October pushes it later. Contacts going home is good news for containment. It is not the all-clear that cautious source markets are waiting for.

For a lodge or hotel owner the practical point is sequencing. Guests, agents and insurers will read "contacts released" as the end. Your reception and sales teams should be able to explain the difference in one sentence, and your 2027 contracts should not assume a clear status before December.

A second date to keep apart: the joint East African visa for AFCON 2027 (19 June to 17 July 2027) is still a proposal, with no fee or operating dates agreed. Price that window on today's visa rules.
