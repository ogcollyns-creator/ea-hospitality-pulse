1️⃣ Africa CDC says Congo's Ebola cases have stopped falling, six months in

**What happened.** Africa CDC's six-month report counts 11,021 confirmed Bundibugyo cases and 5,358 deaths in five countries to 8 Nov. Congo has held at about 460–480 a week since late September; only 11% of cases came from contact lists, against a 95% target *(Africa CDC, 20 Nov)*. Uganda has 20 cases and no active ones *(CDC, updated 19 Nov)*. On 17 Nov Uganda's health ministry told police to keep concerts and mass gatherings suspended in unnamed high-risk border districts "until further notice" *(Nile Post, 19 Nov)*. The US bar on non-citizens recently in Congo, Uganda or South Sudan expires 10 Dec *(Federal Register 2026-23114)*.

**Read.** Inference: cases have levelled off, not fallen, so renewal is likelier than a lapse.

🎯 So what: don't tell US agents the bar ends on 10 Dec. Events in Uganda's high-risk border districts need written ministry clearance.

## LINKEDIN

The US entry bar on visitors from Uganda has an expiry date: 4:59pm EST on 10 December. Lodges should not plan on it.

Africa CDC's six-month report on the Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak, published on 20 November, counts 11,021 confirmed cases and 5,358 deaths in five countries. Congo's weekly count has held at about 460 to 480 since late September. Only 11% of cases came from known contact lists, against a 95% target. Case numbers have levelled off; they are not falling.

Uganda itself has had no active case for months. On 17 November its health ministry still told police to keep concerts and mass gatherings suspended in high-risk border districts until further notice.

Our read: the US order covering Congo, Uganda and South Sudan was last renewed on 10 November, for 30 days. On these numbers, another renewal is more likely than a lapse. That is our inference, not a CDC statement.

For gorilla and western-circuit lodges, the practical step is to tell US agents what the order actually says and when it is reviewed, without promising it will end. If the bar lifts, it is good news. If you have already promised, a renewal becomes a cancellation.
