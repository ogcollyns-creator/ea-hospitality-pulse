## LINKEDIN

Kenya's tourism budget for 2027/28 is being decided this week, not next June.

Parliament is taking public submissions on the 2027 Budget Policy Statement until 5pm on Friday 20 November. The statement sets spending ceilings for every ministry and agency, with total spending proposed at KSh5.323 trillion and KSh1.321 trillion of borrowing (Parliament notice, reported by Kenyans.co.ke on 16 November).

Two things make this round different. The budget will be read and passed about three months earlier than usual, because of the August 2027 election. And once the ceilings are set, a sector can only fight over its share within them.

The industry already knows what it wants. The Kenya Association of Hotelkeepers and Caterers said this month that destination marketing needs predictable funding for the Kenya Tourism Board.

Our read: the useful submission is short. One page, one ask, with the arrivals and earnings case attached. Associations should file it by Friday, and owners of larger groups can file in their own names too.

If the ceiling for tourism marketing is set without the sector's view, the June debate will be about crumbs.
