**Kenya confirmed its first imported Ebola case on 6 October: a passenger who flew Entebbe–Nairobi on Jambojet on 3 October and died in Nairobi**
_🌆 Evening Wrap · Wed 7 Oct 2026_

━━━━━━━━━
1️⃣ Kenya's first Ebola case came through Uganda, on a scheduled flight, past airport screening

A Kenyan who had lived in the DRC for seven years travelled by road to Kampala, then flew Jambojet JM 8523 Entebbe–Nairobi, landing at JKIA at 13:10 on Sat 3 Oct. He cleared routine Port Health screening, was admitted to Nairobi Hospital's isolation wing and died late on 5 Oct *(Health CS Aden Duale, MoH statement via APO Group, 7 Oct; Kenyans.co.ke, 6–7 Oct; some outlets give 6 Oct)*. First count: 28 contacts, plus 23 passengers and 4 crew being traced. By 7 Oct, 57 contacts and 10 in quarantine; surveillance at 15 entry points *(PS Mary Muthoni via Kenyans.co.ke, 7 Oct)*. Her line: "We do not have an outbreak; we have an imported case." WHO advises against travel or trade restrictions on Kenya, Uganda or DRC *(Tedros, via People Daily, 7 Oct)*. health.go.ke could not be opened; figures are the ministry's, as relayed.
Our inference: what failed is screening, not borders. DG Patrick Amoth suggested medication may have masked fever; unproven. Uganda was declared Ebola-free on 27 Aug.
🎯 So what: send agents a one-page fact sheet tonight (one case, no local transmission, your screening steps). Offer free date changes; do not cut rate.

2️⃣ The UK did not raise Kenya's level on 7 October, only added Ebola detail

FCDO's Kenya page was updated 7 Oct with "new information about the detection of Ebola and measures taken by the Kenyan Government". The no-go areas are unchanged *(gov.uk, read 7 Oct)*. The Star reports it notes enhanced screening for arrivals from Uganda, Ethiopia and DRC *(The Star, 7 Oct)*. Canada's Kenya page has no Kenya-specific Ebola alert *(travel.gc.ca, 7 Oct)*. The US entry order covers DRC, Uganda and South Sudan, not Kenya, and lapses 11 Oct unless renewed; we found no renewal *(CDC, 11 Sep order)*. Germany and France not reached.
🎯 So what: tell UK agents the level did not move, and quote the line. Check US-market agents separately before 11 Oct.

Government Delivery Unit figures put arrivals at 2,792,875 and earnings at Sh564bn to June 2026 (period basis not stated); international MICE delegates 38,705 *(The Star, 6 Oct)*. Our reading: a perception problem erodes city MICE and short-lead leisure first.
🎯 So what: watch new-booking pace, not cancellations.

## LINKEDIN

Kenya has its first Ebola case, and the screening that was meant to stop it did not.

On 6 October, Health CS Aden Duale confirmed an imported case of Bundibugyo virus. The patient, a Kenyan who had lived in the DRC for seven years, went by road to Kampala, flew Jambojet from Entebbe, and landed at JKIA on 3 October. He passed routine screening and died in Nairobi on 5 October. By 7 October, 57 contacts had been identified and 10 quarantined. The ministry's position: an imported case, not an outbreak. WHO advises against travel or trade restrictions.

The UK updated its Kenya advice on 7 October. The level did not change.

The inference is ours. The risk to operators is not infection. It is the question agents ask first: is Kenya still safe to send people to? Guests whose insurance and governments still allow travel will not be moved by a discount; they respond to a clear fact sheet and flexible dates.

Do three things this week. Brief agents in writing. Offer free date changes before anyone asks for a refund. Track new bookings, not cancellations.
