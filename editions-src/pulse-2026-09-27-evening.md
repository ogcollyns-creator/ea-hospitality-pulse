## LINKEDIN

On Thursday, Tanzania starts refusing entry to foreign visitors without a US$44 insurance policy. The rule has been gazetted since 4 September. What changed this weekend is that it now has a start date and a seller.

A Ministry of Finance public notice, published by the Tanzania Association of Tour Operators on 26 September, fixes 1 October 2026. Visitors arriving on the mainland buy from the National Insurance Corporation. Those arriving through Zanzibar buy from the Zanzibar Insurance Corporation. The policy lasts 92 days and allows multiple entries.

The notice works by entry point, not by itinerary. It doesn't settle whether a guest landing at Kilimanjaro who ends the trip on the beach also owes Zanzibar's own US$44. Lawyers at Clyde & Co read the exemption as covering EAC and SADC residents; most press reports say citizens. For lodges selling long weekends to Nairobi's expatriate market, that distinction is the price.

Package tours generated 75.2% of Tanzania's 2025 tourism earnings, so most guests will hear about this from a DMC, not from you. Brief your partners by arrival airport this week, and get the combined-itinerary answer in writing before a guest meets it at the gate.
