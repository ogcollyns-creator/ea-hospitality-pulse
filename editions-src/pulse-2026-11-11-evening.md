**Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient on 11 November and has set 23 December for its Ebola-free declaration**
_🌆 Evening Wrap · Wed 11 Nov 2026_

━━━━━━━━━
1️⃣ Kenya's 42-day Ebola countdown started today; the target date is 23 December

**What happened.** Health CS Aden Duale said the last patient left treatment clear of the virus on 11 Nov, and the final 11 contacts were released. The tally is nine cases, two deaths and seven recoveries. Kenya is on course for an Ebola-free declaration on 23 Dec if no new case appears *(KBC; Kenyans.co.ke, 11 Nov)*. Duale said Kenya will issue no "Ebola-free" travel certificates, will not quarantine travellers for other countries, and is "open for business".

**Read.** Inference: 23 Dec is too late to rescue December; it helps January to March bookings.

🎯 So what: tell agents Kenya issues no health certificates, so guests should not ask for one. Set any Ebola clause to end on 23 Dec.
🏷 City, Bush, Beach | Kenya | Confirmed | impact:demand

2️⃣ The US renewed its Ebola entry ban for 30 days on 10 November, and Uganda is still on it

The CDC continued its order on non-US nationals recently in the DRC, Uganda or South Sudan from 5pm US Eastern on 10 Nov, for 30 days *(CDC order signed 9 Nov)*. Uganda finished 42-day monitoring on 27 Aug but stays listed over cross-border risk from the DRC. Kenya is not covered and sits at CDC Level 1.

🎯 So what: non-US passport holders, green-card holders included, who visit Uganda still cannot enter the US for 21 days. Check US-resident guests' onward plans; the next decision is due about 10 Dec.

## LINKEDIN

Kenya now has a date: 23 December.

The Health Ministry discharged its last Ebola patient on 11 November. If no new case appears in 42 days, Kenya will be declared Ebola-free on 23 December (KBC, Kenyans.co.ke, 11 November). The outbreak ends with nine cases, two deaths and seven recoveries.

Two details matter more to hotels than the date. Health CS Aden Duale said Kenya will not issue "Ebola-free" certificates for travel, and will not hold travellers to meet another country's entry rules. Guests and agents who ask for a clearance letter should be told plainly that none exists.

The date itself is awkward. The declaration lands two days before Christmas, so it will not save December's softer bookings. Its value is in January to March, when long-haul planners want a clean record before they commit.

Meanwhile the US renewed its Ebola entry order on 10 November for 30 days, and Uganda is still listed despite completing its monitoring in August. Kenya is not covered.

Our read: write cancellation clauses around published dates, 23 December for Kenya and about 10 December for the next US decision, not around headlines.
