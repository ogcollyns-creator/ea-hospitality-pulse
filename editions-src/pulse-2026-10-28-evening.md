2️⃣ DRC's Ebola outbreak passed 10,000 confirmed cases, with 10,060 cases and 4,887 deaths at 25 October
That is 423 more cases and 241 more deaths than WHO's 19 October count we cited on 22 Oct, though the sources differ. Ituri holds 71.7% of cases; 66 health zones in 8 of 26 provinces are affected. The report lists DRC, Uganda, Kenya and Nigeria, plus one imported case in France *(Xinhua via The Independent Uganda, 27 Oct, citing DRC health authorities)*. Uganda itself says it has no active cases.
Our inference: the source outbreak is still growing, so Kenya's 42-day clock is the only part of this on a timer.
🎯 So what: price US and European Ebola questions as a months-long issue, not a weeks-long one.
🏷 City, Bush, Beach | Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda | Confirmed | impact:risk

3️⃣ ECDC rates Ebola infection risk for European visitors to Kenya as low, while two US agencies report no Kenya booking impact
ECDC's 27 Oct statement, announcing experts for Nigeria, says infection risk for Europeans living in or travelling to affected areas in Kenya and Nigeria is low with precautions, and risk to the EU very low. It gives no travel restrictions *(ECDC, 27 Oct)*. Two US luxury agencies, Extraordinary Journeys and Greatways Travel, told Tourism Update they see no tangible Kenya impact; no figures given *(Tourism Update, 27 Oct)*.
Our inference: agent comments are anecdote, not data, and the US Level 3 advisory has bitten before.
🎯 So what: quote ECDC's wording to European agents; ask US agents for actual cancellation counts.
