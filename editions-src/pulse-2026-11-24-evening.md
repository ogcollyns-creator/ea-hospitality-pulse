1️⃣ CAF names six AFCON 2027 host cities; Arusha and Zanzibar are in, Kenya's coast and Eldoret are out

**What happened.** CAF confirmed the cities on Saturday: Nairobi (Talanta, 60,000; Kasarani, 48,000); Dar es Salaam, Arusha and Zanzibar; Kampala and Hoima. Matches are not yet allocated to stadiums *(The Standard, 22 Nov; HapaKenya, 23 Nov)*. Tanzania's LOC secretary-general Neema Msitha says CAF's coordination office moves from Nairobi to Dar es Salaam and opens on 25 Nov, which she says "does not guarantee" Tanzania the opener or final *(Pulse Kenya, 23 Nov)*. It runs 19 Jun–17 Jul 2027.

**Read.** Inference: Kenya's room-night share now rests on one city; Zanzibar and Arusha get team and fan demand on the edge of their peak.
🎯 So what: Arusha and Zanzibar hotels, ask your LOC for the hotel brief this week; Nairobi, price any CAF block against July safari transit nights.

2️⃣ Tanzania's insurance regulator tells insurers to pay Nungwi fire claims fairly and on time

TIRA's public notice of 22 Nov, citing Zanzibar's tourism ministry, puts the 21 Nov fire at six hotels, four restaurants and about 40 shops, with 183 guests moved and no casualties. It asks policyholders to notify insurers early and file documents; insurers must guide claimants and settle "fairly and on time" *(TIRA, 23 Nov)*.

**Read.** Inference: claim speed now decides whether damaged hotels reopen for Christmas.
🎯 So what: coast owners, check cover for fire, business interruption and guest relocation, and keep invoices for moved guests.

## LINKEDIN

Kenya will host AFCON 2027 in one city. Tanzania will host it in three.

On Saturday, 21 November, CAF confirmed the tournament's six host cities: Nairobi in Kenya; Dar es Salaam, Arusha and Zanzibar in Tanzania; Kampala and Hoima in Uganda. Nairobi's matches go to the new 60,000-seat Talanta stadium and Kasarani. CAF has not yet said which stadium gets which match. Tanzania's organising committee says CAF's coordination office moves from Nairobi to Dar es Salaam this week.

Our read: this changes the hotel map more than the football map. Kenya's coast and Eldoret are out, so Kenya's tournament demand now sits in Nairobi alone. Arusha and Zanzibar gain team, media and fan demand from 19 June to 17 July 2027, the start of the region's most expensive season.

For owners, the job now is commercial. In Arusha and Zanzibar, ask your organising committee for the hotel requirements before the lists close. In Nairobi, test any tournament room block against what those nights earn from July safari guests passing through.
