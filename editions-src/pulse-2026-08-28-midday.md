1️⃣ THE MAINLAND JUST OVERTOOK ZANZIBAR ON YIELD

For the first time, a visitor night on mainland Tanzania is worth more than a visitor night in Zanzibar: USD 289 against USD 274 (Bank of Tanzania, 2025 International Visitors' Exit Survey, released 13 July 2026). A year earlier the island led, USD 251 to USD 243. Mainland spend rose 19.1%; Zanzibar's rose 9%.

The mechanism sits in the same document. Package tours took 67.2% of Zanzibar's visitors in 2025, up from 61.8% — but their share of the island's earnings fell to 59.1% from 63.6%. Zanzibar's package guests spent USD 379 a night, DOWN from USD 391. That is the only spend line in the survey that moved backwards. Independent travellers went the other way: USD 211, up 14% from USD 185.

Our read, labelled as inference: the island is selling more of its inventory through intermediaries paying less for it. Arrivals growth covered that trade while it lasted. The June 2026 print — 69,605 arrivals, +3.1% year-on-year (Zanzibar Commission for Tourism, 13 July 2026) — says the cover is thinning.

🎯 So what: Count what share of your room-nights went through contracted allocation this year against last. Zanzibar added 5.4 points of package mix and was paid 3% less per night for it.

2️⃣ PACKAGE ISN'T THE PROBLEM. UNDIFFERENTIATED PACKAGE IS.

Same survey: mainland package guests spent USD 479 a night, up from USD 416. By activity, hunting took USD 710.8 (+54%), cultural tourism USD 537 and wildlife safari USD 452 — against birdwatching at USD 105.6. Average stay fell a night on both circuits, to 9 on the mainland and 6 in Zanzibar.

🎯 So what: Specialisation prices; a substitutable beach night does not. Bush operators can hold rate on specialist product. Everyone lost a night of stay — recover it on rate, not volume.

3️⃣ STILL TRUE — THE ADVISORY SPLIT ON ZANZIBAR

The US has held Tanzania and Zanzibar at Level 3 "Reconsider travel" since 31 October 2025, with no Zanzibar carve-out (verified against travel.state.gov, 4 August 2026). The UK has no advisory against travel. Zanzibar's two biggest source markets are Italy at 18.8% and France at 10.6%.

🎯 So what: Don't discount a European base against an American advisory that doesn't bind it.
