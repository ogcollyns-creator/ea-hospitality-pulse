## LINKEDIN

Zanzibar is adding more branded beach rooms in 2026 than in any year in its history. Many independent resorts are pricing as if it isn't happening.

The 272-room Catalonia Grand, Le Méridien Zanzibar and the 360-room Iberostar Selection Muyuni all open this year, with Ritz-Carlton Nungwi to follow. And this pipeline is being delivered, not just announced — W Hospitality Group's 2026 data shows 77.5% of Tanzania's chain pipeline already under construction, among the highest ratios in Africa.

The second-order effect owners miss: these brands arrive with global loyalty programmes and charter relationships already attached. They don't need to discount to fill — they redirect demand at the source, in Madrid, Paris and Warsaw.

The move smart independents make now: lock 2027 charter and tour-operator allocations before the new inventory contracts them, and invest in what a brand cannot replicate — site, character and the guests who come back for you.
