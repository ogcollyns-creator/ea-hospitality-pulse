Airport traffic and airline route events

Seats decide arrivals, and arrivals decide occupancy. True seats-per-week data sits behind commercial aviation databases, so nothing is published here that cannot be verified. What is tracked instead is official airport traffic, which is authoritative but lagging, and confirmed route events, which are forward-looking and actionable.
