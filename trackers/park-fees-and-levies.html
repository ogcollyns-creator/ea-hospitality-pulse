EU green-claims rules — Directive (EU) 2024/825 (EmpCo)
Bans generic environmental claims — 'eco-friendly', 'green', 'sustainable', 'responsible' — unless a recognised certification directly covers the claim.Bans generic environmental claims — 'eco-friendly', 'green', 'sustainable', 'responsible' — unless a recognised certification directly covers the claim. Bans 'carbon neutral' and 'climate compensated' where the claim rests on offsets outside the value chain. Bans self-created labels and badges. Broad claims survive only under an EU Ecolabel or an EN ISO 14024 Type I ecolabel. Schemes named as meeting the third-party verification bar include Travelife, Green Key, Green Globe, EarthCheck and Green Destinations. PRACTICAL POINT: the penalty exposure sits with the EU trader — the tour operator, package organiser or OTA reselling you — under the Unfair Commercial Practices Directive that EmpCo amends, which carries maximum fines of at least 4% of annual turnover in the member states concerned.
