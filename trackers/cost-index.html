The cost side: fuel, currency, energy and inflation

Rate is only half of a margin. These are the input costs that decide the other half — fuel and diesel, the shilling and the franc, energy tariffs and the inflation running underneath them. Every figure carries the official source it came from and the period it covers, because several of these series publish on a long lag.
