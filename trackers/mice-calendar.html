TWO CONTRACTING POINTS ADDED THIS MORNING. First, AFRAA's August 2026 capacity table puts JKIA sixth in Africa at 567,644 seats against Addis Ababa Bole's 1.25 million, with intra-African seat capacity up 10.5% year on year and Eastern Africa holding 22.7% of continental capacity: the delegate arriving for a Nairobi congress is increasingly routed on an African carrier and increasingly from within Africa, which argues for a regional corporate and MICE rate rather than a long-haul one. Second, the US Federal Reserve's 16 September rise to 3.75-4.00% raises the cost of the USD facilities behind the region's convention-adjacent hotel pipeline, so any 2027 venue or block commitment underwritten on cheap dollar debt should be re-tested before signature.
