Accuracy, and the limits of it

We take verification seriously and publish our methodology openly at methodology.html. Every dataset carries its sources, its sample size and its confidence grade, and we mark figures as measured, reported or early signal rather than presenting them all as equally solid.

That said, we are a small publication reporting on markets where official data is thin, delayed, or absent. We cannot warrant that everything is accurate, complete or current. Specifically:

  • Rates are observations of publicly displayed prices at a moment in time, at a fixed booking convention. They are not offers, not guaranteed availability, and not what you will be charged.
  • Pipeline entries reflect announcements. Announced projects are delayed, redesigned and cancelled routinely. Opening dates are operator targets, not commitments.
  • Forecasts in the Foresight Ledger are explicitly uncertain, which is why we score them publicly, including the ones we get wrong.
  • Third-party data is reproduced as published by its source and inherits that source's errors.
If we get something wrong, tell us. We correct errors openly rather than quietly — corrections are logged with what was wrong, what is right, and when it changed. Email ceo@eahospitalitypulse.com.
