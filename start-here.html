Or, in order, if you'd rather just follow the steps

  1. Read one edition. Click "Today's edition" above, or open any card in the archive — no signup needed to read.
  2. Subscribe on whichever platform you'll actually check. Telegram gets the full edition; WhatsApp gets a 15-second skim; LinkedIn gets a longer analytical piece most evenings.

  4. Skim the Methodology page once, so you know what "Confirmed", "Reported" and "unconfident" mean when you see them.
