The week the risk map flipped — from contagion to cost.

Read the daily briefs back-to-back and it looks like a health-and-security week: Ebola, a US entry ban, a Kenya advisory re-issue. But those are the fading risks. Uganda was declared Ebola-free on 28 July, 30 days early. The Kenya advisory that spooked agents turned out to be a feed-level re-issue of a March 2025 page. These are demand-side scares with a short half-life.

The story that firmed up this week is quieter and far more durable: your cost base. Kenya's inflation hit a two-year high, and it is diverging from a benign Tanzania and Uganda. That divergence lands at the exact moment a weak-rand South Africa is selling the same Big Five experience 30–60% cheaper. The headline risk is transient. The margin risk is structural — and nobody put those two side by side day-to-day.

THE WEEK'S SIGNALS

1️⃣ Kenya inflation rose to 6.5% in July — a near two-year high, driven by electricity, transport (15.6%) and food (9.0%).
🎯 So what: your single biggest 2027 threat isn't a headline, it's a cost base climbing toward the CBK's 7.5% ceiling. Model wage and utility creep now.
🏷 KNBS via Bloomberg / The Star, 31 July 2026

2️⃣ Uganda declared Ebola-free (28 July) — yet DRC's outbreak hit 3,605 confirmed cases, 1,587 deaths, 44% CFR across 49 health zones.
🎯 So what: the US ban won't decouple Uganda from DRC on epidemiology. Treat the 12 Aug renewal as a regional-contagion tax, not a Uganda verdict.
🏷 WHO AFRO Situation Report 11 / Uganda MoH, 28–30 July 2026

3️⃣ Airlift keeps expanding: RwandAir went to a 4th weekly Kigali–Paris–Brussels rotation from 1 August; Kenya Airways took Nairobi–Gatwick from 3 to 5 weekly.
🎯 So what: seats are growing faster than rate discipline. More capacity into the same demand pool pressures lead-in fares — protect ADR before it's competed away.
🏷 RwandAir / Kenya Airways schedules, effective July–1 Aug 2026

🔭 THE DEEP DIVE — Competing destinations
With the rand weak, comparable Big Five safari runs 30–60% cheaper in South Africa than in Kenya or Tanzania for the same lodge tier; Kruger's day fee sits near US$33. As EA's cost base rises, that price gap widens on its own.
🎯 So what: you are not only competing with the camp down the river — you're competing with a currency. Sell scarcity, guiding and access, not a rate card that can't win on price.

📡 30/90-DAY DEMAND CALENDAR

• 12–13 Aug — US CDC s.362 Ebola order renewal decision. → Brief agents now; assume the ban persists.
• 15 Aug — EPRA fuel price review (Kenya). → Lock August transfer & generator quotes before the 15th.
• ~28 Aug — Uganda completes its 42-day EVD all-clear. → Have "Uganda is clear" collateral ready to fire.
• Early Sept — KNBS & UBOS August CPI land. → This is the cost-gap number to quote in rate negotiations.
• 25–27 Sept — Uganda park free-entry days. → Package add-on nights around the waiver.
• 25 Oct — Yas Zanzibar Marathon (~10,000 runners). → Release event rates and minimum-stays now.
• Aug–Oct — Mara/Serengeti peak crossing. → Hold rate discipline; don't discount into peak.
