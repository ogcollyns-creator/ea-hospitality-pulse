1️⃣ FORECAST CHECK-IN — FOUR DAYS TO 12 AUGUST, AND THE REAL QUESTION IS UGANDA
The US CDC's §362 Ebola entry suspension (DRC, Uganda, South Sudan) still expires Tuesday 12 August. Re-checked this morning: the latest CDC order remains the one published 16 July (effective 13 July) — no early renewal, no early lapse. The DRC epidemiology behind it hardened again: 4,053 confirmed cases and 1,850 deaths to 5 August (ECDC, updated 7 Aug), up from 3,874 a week earlier.
But watch the split. DRC's numbers argue for renewal (P044/P047). Uganda's argue the other way — Ebola-free since 28 July, last case 21 June, now 48 days clear. The operator question on 12 Aug is not "renew or lift" — it's whether CDC decouples Uganda from DRC and drops it from the list (P035, our early signal).
🎯 So what: keep the ban in your base case past 12 Aug — but if you run Uganda bush or Entebbe city rooms, 12 Aug is the date a US-source recovery could get its first green light. Have your trade-partner note drafted for either outcome.
