Expert Brief: East Africa keeps announcing record arrivals.

🌅 EA HOSPITALITY PULSE — Mon 10 Aug, Morning
Quiet news morning. One Expert Brief, then the board.

1️⃣ THE RECORD THAT HIDES A RISK — READ YOUR SOURCE-MARKET MIX, NOT YOUR ARRIVALS LINE

Every headline this year says the same thing: records. Zanzibar cleared 917,167 arrivals in 2025 (OCGS). Tanzania banked US$4.41bn in tourism earnings, up 13% (NBS, 2025). Kenya reports ~7.9m visitors. The applause is deserved. It is also the wrong thing to be watching.

Look underneath Zanzibar's boom and you find a single load-bearing pillar: Europe supplied 61.9% of June arrivals (43,105 of 69,605) and 69.5% in January (OCGS, June figure released ~12 Jul). One region, most of your bookings. That is not a franchise — it's a bet on one continent's disposable income and one continent's risk appetite.

What everyone is missing: a record-arrivals headline is not validation, it's a concentration warning. And you don't have to imagine the shock — it's live. Uganda sits at US Level 4 "Do Not Travel" (State Dept, 5 Aug) and the US CDC Section 362 entry order (renewed 13 Jul, next call ~12 Aug) has effectively frozen one Western source market for the Uganda/DRC corridor overnight. A single government notice did that. A European recession would do the same to a coast that leans 62–70% on Europe.

The hedge already works — someone's proven it. Tanzania grew Chinese arrivals from 33,000 to 82,000 in two years through Mandarin-language outreach. Deliberate diversification, not luck.

🎯 So what, by segment
🏖 Beach: most exposed. Build Gulf/India/intra-Africa shoulder fill now — language, OTA and payment rails ready before the next Western wobble, not after.
🦁 Bush: the Uganda freeze shows how fast one advisory empties a source market. Court Gulf/India feeder demand on the new Doha/Dubai lift; hold rate through a Western gap rather than discounting.
🏙 City: MICE and intra-African business travel are your natural diversifier — MKTE (Oct), FHS Africa Uganda 2027, ICCA Kigali 2027 are demand that doesn't depend on Western leisure sentiment.
